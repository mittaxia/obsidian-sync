Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

1. Purpose

This application is intended solely for personal use, synchronizing Obsidian notes across the owner's devices through the GSync plugin and Google Drive API.

2. Data Access

The application uses Google OAuth to authorize access to Google Drive. The requested permissions may allow access to Google Drive files. The intended use is to read, create, update, and delete files for note synchronization.

3. Data Usage and Storage

Files are synchronized between Google Drive and the owner's devices. The owner does not sell personal data or intentionally transfer it to third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

This setup relies on Google Drive, Google OAuth, Obsidian, and the third-party GSync plugin. These services and software have their own data handling practices.

5. Revoking Access

Google Drive access can be revoked at any time through the Google Account security settings.

The intended use of Google user data is limited to personal note synchronization, subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy.
